    Grammar BM — Last-Minute Guide

    1 · Non-finites: decide by governor + meaning

    PatternUseExample

    stop to dopause in order to actstop to smoke
    remember doingpast memoryremember meeting her
    remember to dofuture obligationremember to call

    Always: preposition + -ing; make/let/have + object + bare infinitive; look forward to + -ing; had better/would rather + bare infinitive.

    2 · Tense & aspect triggers

    Simple present

    habit, truth, performative, timetable, future time/if clause, state verb.

    Present progressive

    ongoing, temporary, annoyance with always, arranged future.

    Present perfect

    unspecified past, current result, period leading to now.

    Present perfect progressive

    duration/activity/repetition or visible secondary result.

    Past progressive

    background, interruption, simultaneity, temporary past habit.

    Past perfect

    earlier event before an established past point.

    3 · Future and conditional map

    will spontaneous decision / factual prediction going to present intention / visible evidence present progressive arranged future simple present timetable / future sub-clause

    4 · Modal meaning first, form second

    must deduction / speaker authority have to external obligation can’t have past impossibility had better urgent advice + consequence was able to specific fulfilled past ability needn’t have happened but was unnecessary be to order / formal plan / fate be supposed to rumour / expected duty

    Critical: mustn’t = prohibition; don’t have to = no obligation. may not = possibly not or no permission; cannot = impossibility.

    5 · Relative clauses

    Defining

    No commas. that/which/who possible. Zero only when the pronoun is the object.

    Non-defining

    Commas. No that and no zero pronoun. Can add removable information.

    • Fronted preposition: to whom / about which — never to that.
    • Partitive: ten people, few of whom… / five books, all of which…
    • Connective which: can refer to the whole preceding clause.
    • What: contains its own antecedent = “that which”.

    6 · Word order and inversion

    Normal mid position

    before lexical verb; after be; after first auxiliary.

    Degree/manner

    after all auxiliaries: has been completely revised.

    End order

    manner – place – time; after motion: place – manner – time.

    Inversion

    negative/restrictive fronting: Never have I… Only then did I…

    Simple verb: add do/does/did + base form. Compound verb: move the existing first auxiliary. No inversion after no doubt, nevertheless or not long after.

    7 · Determiner traps

    few hardly any count nouns a few some count nouns little hardly any uncountable a little some uncountable go to court institution go to the court specific place his hand possessive body part hit him in the hand affected body part

    8 · Translation checklist

    1. Underline the grammatical target before translating.
    2. Build the verb phrase first: tense + aspect + modal + non-finite form.
    3. Check clause structure: conditional sequence, reported speech, relative case and commas.
    4. Check inversion after never/nowhere/only + fronted phrase.
    5. Check articles, countability, possessives and natural collocations.
    6. Read the complete English sentence once for meaning and once for form.
